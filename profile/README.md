### 🛡️ Mission

**The-Andb** is building the next generation of database orchestration tools. We bridge the gap between complex SQL schemas and agile development workflows, providing developers with high-performance engines and intuitive visual interfaces to manage migrations, comparisons, and deployments with zero friction.

### 💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. Can my company use TheAndb for free in our daily operations?

**Yes, absolutely.** Your company can download and use TheAndb for free for any internal commercial purpose, including managing client databases, running migrations, and optimizing your own infrastructure.

#### 2. What exactly is prohibited under this license?

You are only restricted from using TheAndb to compete with us. Specifically, you cannot take our source code, rebrand/white-label it to sell it as your own product, or host it as a paid, managed database orchestration SaaS.

#### 3. When would we need to purchase an Enterprise License?

You only need to contact us for a paid license if your team grows and requires enterprise-level compliance and management tools, such as Centralized Authentication (SSO/SAML), Audit Logging (tracking who modified which database), or Dedicated Priority Support.

### 🧩 The Ecosystem (Core Repositories)

Our platform is composed of specialized modules, each serving a critical role in the orchestration pipeline:

#### 🧠 The Core Engines

- **[andb-core](https://github.com/The-Andb/andb-core)** (Current): The modern brain. A stateless, high-performance logic library written in Framework/TypeScript. Handles SQL parsing, dependency graphing, and migration strategies.
- **[andb-core-legacy](https://github.com/The-Andb/andb-core-legacy)** (Legacy): The original engine node.js implementation. Maintained for backward compatibility.

#### 🎨 The User Interfaces

- **[andb-desktop](https://github.com/The-Andb/andb-desktop)** (Current): The flagship desktop application. Built with Electron, Vue 3, and Vite. Makes schema comparison and conflict resolution as easy as a Git merge.
- **[andb-desktop-legacy](https://github.com/The-Andb/andb-desktop-legacy)** (Legacy): The previous generation UI.

#### 🛠️ The Tools & Utilities

- **[andb-cli](https://github.com/The-Andb/andb-cli)**: The bridge to CI/CD. A powerful terminal utility for automating exports, imports, and environment synchronizations.
- **[andb-www](https://github.com/The-Andb/andb-www)**: The public face of our product. A high-performance marketing site built with Vue 3.

#### 📂 Infrastructure

- **[andb-monorepo](https://github.com/The-Andb/andb-monorepo)**: The Monorepo root. Orchestrates documentation, docker configurations, and high-level planning.
- **[.github](https://github.com/The-Andb/.github)**: Organization-wide health files, templates, and profiles.
